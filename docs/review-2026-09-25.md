# Design review, 2026-09-25

An independent reviewer (a separate agent briefed as a senior EE, told to be
skeptical and to change nothing) checked the circuit against the datasheets:
RP2350 datasheet and hardware-design guide, the RPi minimal KiCad reference,
and the CH334, AMS1117, WS2812D, 1N4148W, EC12E, USB-C, USB-A, W25Q128JV,
ABM8 and AOTA inductor datasheets.

**Result:** no wiring errors. Every IC and connector pin landed on the right net.
The findings, and what was done about each:

| # | Finding | Severity | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Core-regulator inductor L1 would be placed reversed. RPi wants the polarity dot on +1V1; the easyeda2kicad footprint marks the dot on pad 2 | Should fix | **Fixed**: pad 2 now goes to +1V1. Check the dot in JLCPCB's placement preview |
| 2 | First WS2812 powered through a diode is marginal: VIH margin ~0 V at 5.25 V VBUS, and under-voltage at full white with a sagging VBUS | Should fix | **Fixed**: 2N7002 + 1 kΩ pull-up level shifter (Basic parts), all LEDs on 5 V. Data is inverted in firmware |
| 3 | AMS1117: 1.1–1.3 V dropout means brown-out on VBUS sag (hot-plug); only rated stable with tantalum caps | Should fix | **Fixed**: back to ME6211 (+1 Extended part) |
| 4 | 22 Ω instead of RPi's required 27 Ω USB series resistors | Should fix | **Accepted**: works at full speed; 27 Ω is an Extended part |
| 5 | ~76 µF on upstream VBUS (USB 2.0 allows 10 µF inrush); no fuse on the USB-A VBUS; CH334R reports self-powered | Should fix | **Accepted** for a hobby board: relies on the PC port's protection. Common in cheap hubs |
| 6 | CH334's 6 kV is HBM, not IEC 61000-4-2; USB-A ports get touched | Should fix | **Accepted** for now; USBLC6-2SC6 (C2827654) is the cheap upgrade |
| 7 | J4 lines 20–26 went to GPIO40–46, which aren't 5 V tolerant | Should fix | **Fixed**: moved to GPIO14–20 |
| 8 | Second 4.7 µF on +1V1 away from the LX loop | Nice to have | **Added** |
| 9 | RUN on a test pad for debug-probe reset | Nice to have | **Added** (TP5) |
| 10 | Encoder RC filter | Nice to have | Firmware debounce instead |
| 11 | RP2350-E9 (A2 stepping): pull-downs/floating inputs misbehave; **pull-ups are fine** | Info | Buttons, encoder and matrix all use pull-ups. Never scan the matrix with pull-downs |

Verified OK (highlights): RP2350B power/ground pins and ePad, VREG and crystal
circuits vs the RPi reference; CH334R all 16 pins, 3.3 V on V5 allowed
(§4.2), crystal load (internal ~16 pF, ~10 pF effective) matches the
ABM8-272-T3; flash, crystal, SOT-223/SOT-23 pin orders; USB-C both D± pairs
and 5.1 kΩ CC; USB-A pinout; WS2812 pin order vs footprint; tact switch pad
pairing; symbol pin numbers vs footprint pad numbers for every JLC part;
capacitor voltage ratings.
